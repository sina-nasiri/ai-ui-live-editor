# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## What that means here

**Do:** ask questions, disagree with reasoning attached, say "I don't know",
help people who are new to Laravel or to accessibility work, and accept that
someone else's workflow may be different from yours.

**Don't:** demean people, derail threads, dismiss a report because you
personally cannot reproduce it, or use anyone's inexperience as a punchline.

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. That includes sustained disruption,
unwelcome personal attention, and demeaning comments about anyone's identity —
in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere else this project has a
presence.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled privately. The maintainer may warn, temporarily block, or permanently
ban anyone whose behaviour makes this a worse place to work.

This applies to everyone, maintainers included.
